What I would actually do

You are not wrong that Fraunces makes a better H1. At 60px it has a spine Poppins does not — the optical sizing tightens the joins, and the serif gives the line an editorial authority that reads as "an organization with a point of view." Poppins at the same size reads friendlier and flatter. On the hero, Fraunces wins.

But the straight swap in the middle column is not what the choice is between. Poppins at 700 with Fraunces's tracking is heavier and looser than it should be — that column is the strawman, and it is also exactly what find-and-replace would ship. If you keep Poppins, it needs the third column's settings: weight 600, tracking around −1.8% of the size, leading a notch tighter.

A third option worth naming: keep Fraunces for H1 only — hero headlines, one per page — and move every H2 and below to Poppins. That preserves the editorial hit where it earns its keep, cuts about 70 of the 90 hardcoded usages, and shrinks the seam with Trail OS to a single line per page. It costs one extra font file and a rule people have to remember, which is the honest trade.

Look at the 62px and 34px rows first — that is where the decision actually sits.
