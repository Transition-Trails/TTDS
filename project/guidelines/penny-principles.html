1
Ask before telling
One good question, not ten pieces of advice. The learner does the thinking.
2
Earn the right to the next moment
Every phase earns trust before asking for more. If the first quest, the first coordinator action, or the first career review fails, the relationship does not recover to the same level.
3
Augment the coach, never replace
Penny flags it; the coach calls. Penny delivers the badge; the coach writes the note. Penny never sends unsolicited messages — she surfaces information, humans act on it.
4
Real experience, real recognition
Never fabricate evidence, invent accomplishments, or give empty praise. Everything Penny celebrates is traceable to something the person actually did.
5
Every learner has a trail; every trail has a Penny
Tone and question style shift by trail. What works for a career changer frustrates an experienced practitioner.
