# Founder voices

Org voice is the default and covers everything the organization publishes. A
**founder voice** applies only where a named person signs it: LinkedIn,
Substack, conference talks, a note in a newsletter, a grant narrative with a
byline.

Profiles live as cards in the **Voice** group of the Design System tab.

| Founder | Role | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Angela Landrith | CEO & co-founder | Written — `guidelines/voice-angela-landrith.html` |
| Kim Barnes | COO | Not written — intake questions in `guidelines/voice-kim-barnes.html` |
| Hugh Richardson | CTO | Not written — intake questions in `guidelines/voice-hugh-richardson.html` |

**Placeholders are deliberately empty.** A guessed founder voice is worse than a
missing one: it reads plausibly, so someone writes against it, and the person
whose name is on it never said any of it. The two unwritten profiles carry the
intake questions instead. Twenty minutes of the person answering out loud is
enough to write from.

## What never changes, whoever is speaking

US English · no emoji · never "Trailhead", "Trailblazer" or "Ohana" · the
learner is still the hero even in a story about the founder · no live prices,
dates, or seat counts — those live in the record.

**The test:** if you swapped the byline, would anyone notice? For org voice, no.
For founder voice, immediately — otherwise it was not worth signing.

## Angela — source and one discrepancy

Two sources exist and they do not fully agree:

1. **Notion — [Angela's Writing Profile · Voice & Style Guide](https://app.notion.com/p/37004eb4bf0281eb90f1fc3a28c9e158)**

2. **The `my-writing-style` skill** attached to this project.

**Reconciled 2026-08-12.** The skill carried three things the Notion page did not;
all three were pulled in and written back to Notion, so the two now agree:

- **Funders and grant makers** — Notion had this marked *"(To be developed —
  placeholder)"*. Now written: professional but still human, not robotic, impact
  and story before numbers.
- **Core themes** — the six named themes (Building While Learning · The
  Experience Gap · Builders Are Builders · If I Can Build It, Anybody Can ·
  Betting On Yourself · AI as a Tool, Not Magic).
- **The house metaphor** — the 97-year-old Oak Cliff renovation as the signature
  thread, the same story as a tech career told in two registers.

Notion remains the source of truth. If the two drift again, Notion wins and this
card gets regenerated from it.
