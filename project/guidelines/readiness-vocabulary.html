"Live" requires evidence, not a label. These four words appear across the platform and in every document. Using them precisely is what lets anyone trust a status without re-checking it.

Implemented
The code, authorization, persistence and workflow behavior all exist and work.
Pilot or gated
The workflow exists but is limited by scope, release, or validation gates.
Configuration-dependent
Behavior needs external credentials, scopes, metadata, or a healthy connector.
Planned
The production contract or the operating policy is not complete yet.
These four fills are the canonical status colors — all clear 3:1 on Trail Light, so they stay readable at indicator size. Never use a divider or border token as a status fill. Applies to design artifacts too: a screen in a UI kit showing a gated workflow should say so. A mock that implies something ships is the same lie as a status that does.
