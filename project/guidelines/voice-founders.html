Org voice — the default
Anything the organization publishes: kits, program pages, Trail OS, client documents. Calm, credible, encouraging; the learner is the hero. "You" to the learner, "we" as the org. No first-person singular.
Founder voice — signed by a person
LinkedIn, Substack, conference talks, a note in a newsletter, a grant narrative with a name on it. "I" is allowed, and specificity is the point. Written closer to how that person talks than how they were trained to email.
What never changes, whoever is speaking: US English · no emoji · never "Trailhead", "Trailblazer" or "Ohana" · the learner is still the hero, even in a story about the founder · no live prices, dates, or seat counts — those live in the record.
The test: if you swapped the byline, would anyone notice? Org voice, no. Founder voice, immediately — otherwise it is not worth signing.
