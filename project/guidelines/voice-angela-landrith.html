Always
Start with the story, never the title. Prose over bullets. Short sentences land harder. Lead with self-taught credibility — 22 years in corporate banking, then a Salesforce build; that history is the authority, not a caveat to it.
Never
Over-polished corporate tone. Bullet-point overload — it should read like writing, not a deck. Jargon without explanation. Press-release or grant-application register when talking to real people. Perfect over good.
Learners & tech partners
Peer-level, straight-talking. “Here is what I did, here is what you can do.” Assumes intelligence, not prior knowledge.
Nonprofit clients
Consultative and trust-building, educational without condescending. Empowerment, not dependency.
Funders & grant makers
Professional but still human, not robotic. Impact and story before numbers — they are reading dozens of these.
Core themes — Building While Learning · The Experience Gap · Builders Are Builders · Betting On Yourself · AI as a Tool, Not Magic. The Product Manager principle — answer the real question behind the question, not the one that was asked.
