Trail OS is Transition Trails' proprietary operating platform — Salesforce-native, hosted on Replit. It is at once an internal operating system, a teaching laboratory, a client services platform, and coaching infrastructure. TT runs on Trail OS. The founders are learners too.

Program Map
The staff app. Operations, programs, knowledge, collaboration, governance, administration — where the organization is run.
Homebase
The people surfaces: learner, coach, volunteer, team and DevOps, each on audience-specific routes with their own permissions.
Mobile & extension
An Expo native app plus a browser extension. Both built; release validation is still a gate.
Penny
The intelligence layer, not a feature inside the app. Every surface goes through one server entry point — that single door keeps her voice, permissions and policy identical everywhere.
Data authority
Salesforce owns people, programs, cases, DevOps work, production assets. Postgres owns workflow history, audit, evidence, versions. Nobody owns twice.
The question before adding a table or a field: is this a fact about a person or a program, or is this how the platform behaves?
