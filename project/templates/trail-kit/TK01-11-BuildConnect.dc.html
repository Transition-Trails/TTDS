Connect your email and calendar

12
In Setup, go to Users and open your user. Check that Email shows [Verified] and is the account you'll connect.
You should see: Your email address followed by [Verified].
13
In Setup, go to Permission Sets › Standard Einstein Activity Capture › Manage Assignments. Click Add Assignment, select yourself, click Next, then Assign.
You should see: “1 assignments were successful” and your name in the Assignment Summary.
14
Open your personal Settings from your profile picture, top right. Type Connected in Quick Find and click Email and Calendar Accounts.
You should see: Welcome to Smarter Email and Calendar.
15
Click Connect Account. Read Terms for Sharing Emails and Events, check I've read and understand these terms, and click Next.
You should see: Buttons to connect Google, Office 365 or Exchange.
16
Click Connect My Google Account (or your Microsoft option), sign in and allow access.
You should see: Your account listed as Your Connection, and a banner saying Einstein Activity Capture is getting ready.
Penny, your trail guide
These terms say your emails are shared by default. Who in your org can open the records they'll land on?
Watch out
If you see “You can't connect a new account until your Salesforce admin assigns you to an active Einstein Activity Capture configuration”, the permission set isn't enough. Add the person or their profile in Configurations. For consent messages from Google or Microsoft, go to When it goes wrong.
Remember
The first sync takes a while. Once the banner clears, move on to Verify and run your test script.
