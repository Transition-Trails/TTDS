Before you start

What you'll build

You'll turn on Einstein Activity Capture in a Salesforce org. It quietly logs emails and calendar events to the right records, so nobody has to copy and paste their inbox. You won't just flip a switch. You'll plan it, build it, test it, and leave with proof you can show an employer.

By the end you'll have:
  • A written plan: what syncs, who it covers, and who can see it
  • A working configuration with at least one user assigned
  • A connected email and calendar account
  • A captured email showing on a real record
  • Screenshots and a 90-second explanation for your portfolio
Who this is for
  • New and aspiring Salesforce admins
  • Career changers building hands-on experience
  • Anyone asked to "just turn on activity tracking" who wants to do it right

You don't need a certification. If you can find your way to Setup, you're ready.

Watch out
Einstein Activity Capture only saves an email if it matches a Contact or Lead in Salesforce. Load the test data before you test, or your email will seem to vanish.
Watch out
Connecting a work account? Your IT team may have to approve it first. The Verify tab and the "When consent gets blocked" audio walk you through it.
Pro tip
Your practice org only has room for two users: you and one test user. That's on purpose. You'll plan for a real 40-person team, then build and test with your two. Employers want to see you can think at full size, even when you practice small.
