Load your practice data

Activity capture only saves an email when it matches a Contact or Lead. This file gives you six practice Contacts to match, plus a few traps on purpose.

1
Download trail-kit-01-test-contacts.csv from your kit page.
You should see: Six contacts. Five have an @mailinator.com address.
2
Open the file. In every address, change trailkit01 to a word only you would use, like trailkit01-yourname. Keep @mailinator.com.
You should see: Your word in every address, and Riley and Morgan still sharing one.
3
In Setup, type Data Import Wizard in Quick Find and open it. Click Launch Wizard!
4
Choose Standard objects › Accounts and Contacts › Add new and update existing records, then add your file and follow the steps to Start import.
You should see: Five contacts on Trail Kit Test Org. Note whether Casey, who has no account, came in too.
5
Open one contact and check its email address.
You should see: Your word, spelled exactly as in the file.
What's hidden in this data, on purpose
In the dataWhat it teaches you
Riley and Morgan share one addressWhich record an email attaches to when two could match
Sam has no emailWhat a record that can never match looks like

An address that isn't in the fileFor test 5. Send to trailkit01-yourname-nobody@mailinator.com
Watch out
Only load this into a practice org, never a real company's org.
Pro tip
Mailinator inboxes are public. Anyone who types the name can read them. Pick a word nobody would guess, and send test text only.
Remember
Cleaning up later: every test address ends in @mailinator.com, so a filter on that finds them all.
