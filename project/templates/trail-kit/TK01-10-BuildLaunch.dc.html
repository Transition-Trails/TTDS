Choose who it covers and launch

9
Set Search to Profiles and type System. Move System Administrator [Profile] to Selected. Click Next.
You should see: System Administrator in the Selected list.
10
On Exclude Addresses, enter the domains and addresses you listed in Prepare: Customer Domains, Internal Domains or Email Addresses. Click Next.
You should see: Ready to Launch! with six green checks.
11
Click Finish.
You should see: The Einstein Activity Capture Settings page, with Google G Suite and User-Level OAuth under Connection to Salesforce.
Find your way around later

The Settings page has four tabs: Summary, Configurations, Settings and Excluded Addresses. Come back to Configurations to change who’s covered.

Remember
In a real org, you'd pick the profiles or users from your Northbend plan. In practice, adding your own profile is enough.
Watch out
The screen warns that each user's email must match the email in their Salesforce settings. If they don't match, nothing gets captured.
Pro tip
Check User Health Status under Quick Links shows whether each person is set up. Look there after you connect your account.
