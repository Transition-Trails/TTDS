Reflection

Look back before you move on.

You planned it, built it and tested it. Take ten minutes to write down what you learned while it’s fresh.

1Logic

Why did you set it up this way? Name one decision you made and what it was based on.

For this kit: Why did you choose shared or private for Northbend, and what would change your mind?

2Struggle

Where did you get stuck, and what got you unstuck? What would you tell someone starting this kit?

For this kit: Which test didn’t do what you expected the first time, and what did you check?

3Result

What works now that didn’t before? What proof do you have, and what would you check before using it at work?

For this kit: Which screenshot is your strongest proof, and what would you test again in a real company’s org?
