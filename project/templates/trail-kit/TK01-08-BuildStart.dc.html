Start the setup

1
In Setup, type Einstein Activity Capture in Quick Find. Under Einstein › Einstein Sales › Einstein Activity Capture, click Settings.
You should see: A page titled Welcome To Einstein Activity Capture with a Get Started button.
2
Click Get Started.
You should see: Review terms and give Einstein a try!
3
Read the terms. Check I'm authorized by my company to accept these terms, then continue.
You should see: Select Your Email and Calendar Service.
4
Choose Google G Suite for a Google account or Microsoft Office 365 for Microsoft. Click Next.
You should see: Two choices for how people connect.
5
Choose User Level, so each person connects their own account.
You should see: Name your Configuration.
Watch out
This won't work in a Trailhead Playground. Use a free Developer Edition org.
Penny, your trail guide
That checkbox says you're allowed to accept terms for your company. In a real org, who would you check with before you check it?
Pro tip
Using Microsoft? Choose Microsoft Office 365. If you're not sure which Microsoft option fits, ask whoever runs your email.
