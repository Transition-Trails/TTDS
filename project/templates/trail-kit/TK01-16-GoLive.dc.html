Go-live checklist

Before your feature reaches real users, check each box. Sign off when every item is complete.

Decisions are written down
You recorded what will change and why.
You recorded who made the decision and who owns the feature.
You recorded who can see or use the feature and any information it shows.
Tested
You ran every step in your test script and recorded the results.
You fixed and retested failures or documented why they’re acceptable.
You ran a test meant to fail and checked that the feature handled it as expected.
People are ready
You identified who will be affected.
You told them what will change and when.
You drafted one short announcement that tells people what to do next.
Way back is ready
You wrote down how to undo the change or restore the previous behavior.
You confirmed who can make that call and who will carry it out.
You agreed on what would trigger a rollback.
After go-live
You wrote down what to watch for, including signs that something is wrong.
You confirmed who will check the feature after one day and after one week.
You decided where to record what you find and flag any issues.
For Einstein Activity Capture
Your left-out addresses are entered, and the test for them passed.
You checked data storage after testing, and it’s within your estimate.
Everyone covered has connected their account, and anyone blocked by consent has a request in with IT.
