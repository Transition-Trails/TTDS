About Transition Trails

Tech skills that pay the bills.

Transition Trails is a Texas 501(c)(3) nonprofit. We help people build tech skills that pay the bills, starting with Salesforce administration. Our programs guide small groups through real work, step by step. Every Trail Kit you buy helps fund that work.

Our mission

Empowering individuals and organizations by bridging the experience gap through education, mentorship, and hands-on work.
