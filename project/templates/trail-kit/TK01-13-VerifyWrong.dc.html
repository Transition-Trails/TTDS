When it goes wrong

1. Connecting the account is blocked
Signs: A consent screen says you need an administrator's approval, or the connection is refused.
Try: Stop retrying. This is usually a company setting, not a mistake you made. Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 administrators can control which outside apps may connect. In your practice org, switch to an account you control. At a real company, send your IT team a request using the template on your kit page.
Salesforce Help: “Connect Email and Calendar Accounts to Einstein Activity Capture”
2. Nothing gets captured
Signs: Your test email never shows on the Contact.
Try: Check that the address exactly matches a Contact or Lead. Check that the email on your Salesforce user matches the account you connected. Then give the first sync time before you change anything.
Salesforce Help: “Test, Monitor, or Reset Configurations”
3. Setup is missing, or someone can't connect
Signs: Einstein Activity Capture doesn't appear in Setup. Or a user sees “You can't connect a new account until your Salesforce admin assigns you to an active Einstein Activity Capture configuration.”
Try: Use a Developer Edition org, not a Trailhead Playground. Each person needs two things: the Standard Einstein Activity Capture permission set, and a place in a configuration. Add them or their profile on the Configurations tab.
Salesforce Help: “Give Users Access to Einstein Activity Capture”
4. An email lands on the wrong record
Signs: The email shows on a different Contact than you expected.
Try: Look for two records that share the same address. The test data includes one pair on purpose. Decide which record should own that address and fix the data.
5. A Microsoft 365 account won't connect
Signs: The connection fails or uses an older sign-in method.
Try: Microsoft 365 connections now sign in through Microsoft Graph. Follow Salesforce's steps to move your configuration to it.
Salesforce Help: “Upgrade Microsoft Office 365 Authentication Method to Microsoft Graph”

Watch out
Meeting showing at the wrong time? Salesforce shows events in your Salesforce user's time zone. In our test, a 10:00 a.m. Central meeting showed as 8:00 a.m. because the user was set to Pacific time. Check Time Zone on each user's record in Setup › Users.
