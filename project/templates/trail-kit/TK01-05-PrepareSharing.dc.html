Decide who can see captured activity

Captured email lands on the Contacts, Leads and Accounts it matches. Anyone who can open that record can open the whole email. Decide how much gets captured before anyone connects an inbox.

Watch out
Emails can hold sensitive details about customers, donors or staff. When you're unsure, start with Header Only and capture more later.
What it does not do
It doesn't capture an email unless one of the addresses matches a Contact or Lead in Salesforce.
Remember
Captured email follows your org's activity sharing. In our test org, Activity was set to Private, and a teammate who could see the Contact still opened the full email.
