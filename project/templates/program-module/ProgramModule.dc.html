Why this module exists

Activity data is the first thing a partner organization asks you to be trusted with, and the first place careless configuration causes harm. Your mentor is assessing judgment — the reasoning behind your choices — not your ability to find a settings page.

How this module runs
Week 1Scope with your partner; mentor reviews first.
Week 2Build in the sandbox; supervised activation.
Week 3Present your reasoning; mentor signs off.
Prerequisites
Modules 1–2 signed off · Salesforce Administrator certification · Partner sandbox access granted
Time commitment
3 weeks · approx. 9 supervised hours, plus your own project work
Related Trail Kit
Trail Kit 07 covers the mechanics of this configuration. It is optional reference, not a substitute for the supervised work.
A note on judgment
You will be asked why, not just whether. Keep a record of the decisions you rejected and the reason — that record is often the strongest evidence in your file.

How this module is assessed

Your mentor assesses each criterion at one of three levels using the evidence you submit. Proficient is the standard for sign-off; Exemplary is recorded in your file, and it is what we cite when an employer asks what you can be trusted with.

What Exemplary looked like — Spring 2026 cohort

A learner scoped capture for a food bank's three-person development team, then recommended against enabling calendar sync because donor meetings were held on personal calendars. She documented the reasoning, the partner agreed, and the configuration shipped narrower than originally asked for.

The build was the easy part. The sign-off cited her judgment in declining scope she could have delivered.

Bring the completed evidence to your week 3 review. Your mentor records the level for each criterion and signs off on the following page.
