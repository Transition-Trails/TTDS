Who can do what, and who can see what

Salesforce answers two separate questions with two separate systems. Almost every mistake comes from reaching into the wrong one.

What you can do
Permissions. Edit an account, export a report, run Setup. Granted by profile and permission sets. The tell is a greyed-out button, a field that will not change, or a save that is refused.
What you can see
Record access. Which rows appear at all. Governed by org-wide defaults, the role hierarchy and sharing rules. The tell is a record absent from a list view, or a link that will not open.

Start low, add up
Give the profile the least everyone in that job needs, then add permission sets. Salesforce keeps moving permissions out of profiles — build the habit now.
Never clone per person
A profile per employee is the most common mess we unpick. Forty profiles differing by one checkbox cannot be audited.
Additive means additive
You cannot revoke with a permission set. If someone has too much, it came from their profile or another set — go and find it.
Diagnosing it in one question
"Insufficient privileges" tells you nothing. Salesforce shows it for both halves — a link to a record you cannot see and an action you are not allowed produce the same page. Go by how the person arrived, not by the message.
Could they reach the record and not act on it? Permissions — check the profile, then every permission set they hold. Could they never reach it at all? Record access — check the org-wide default, then the role hierarchy, then sharing rules.
