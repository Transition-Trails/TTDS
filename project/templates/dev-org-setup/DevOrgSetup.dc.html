Set up a free practice org

Never practice in a live org. A Salesforce Developer Edition org is free, permanent, and yours — this is where every Trail Kit expects you to work.

Five steps, about ten minutes
1
Open the signup page
Scan the code above, or type the address. Make sure it is the developer signup, not a trial — trials expire, developer orgs do not.
2
Use an email you will keep
A personal address, not a work one. If you change jobs you keep the org, and you will need that email to reset the password years from now.
3
Pick a username you can read
It must be globally unique and looks like an email but is not one. Something like you.practice@example.dev works.
4
Confirm and set your password
Follow the link in your email. Write the username below — that is the part people lose.
5
Log in once a month
Orgs are deactivated after a long stretch of no logins. Two minutes keeps years of work alive.

What you get
A full org with admin access, sample data, and no expiry date. Every setup screen a paying customer sees, yours to break and rebuild.
What it will not do
Two active users. Enough to test permissions, not enough to simulate a team.
Very little storage. Megabytes, not gigabytes — fine for practice, useless for a real data load.
