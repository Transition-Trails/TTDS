Transition Trails · For nonprofit partnersHow it works

Four conversations, then work starts.

Step 1
Tell us what is stuck
A 30-minute call. No preparation needed.
Step 2
We scope it in writing
One page: the finish line, the risks, what we will not do.
Step 3
You approve, or you don't
Nothing begins until the scope is signed.
Step 4
Work, reviewed weekly
You see progress every week and can stop at any point.
What we ask of you
One named staff contact, about two hours a week. Access to the system in question. Honest feedback when something misses. That is the whole ask.
What we never ask
A fee for the learner's time. A long-term commitment. Access to donor or client data that the project does not genuinely require.
The question everyone asks
"What if the learner is not good enough?" Then the work does not ship. Every change is reviewed against the written scope before it reaches your system, and if a project cannot meet the standard we tell you and we stop. Your risk is the two hours a week, not the outcome.
Who you would be working with
Transition Trails is a nonprofit founded by Angela Landrith. Every project is reviewed against a written scope before anything reaches your system.
If we are not the right fit
We will say so on the first call and point you toward someone better suited. A bad match costs you more than it costs us.
