Change record

Fill this in as you make the change, not afterwards. Six months from now this page is the difference between "someone turned this on" and a decision you can defend.

Date

Made by
Approved by
Why bother
Because the next person to touch this will be a stranger — possibly you, in a year. A change nobody wrote down is a change nobody can safely undo.
"Approved by" does not need a committee. It needs one other person who knew before it happened and would say so. This one page is the habit that separates people who configure systems from people who click in them. It is also the artifact interviewers ask to see.
