Finding your way around Setup

Every kit says "in Setup, type this into Quick Find." If that sentence has ever lost you, this page is the one to read first. Nothing here is specific to one feature.

The four places you will be sent
Setup
Gear icon, top right
The admin area — everything that changes how the org behaves. Click the gear, then Setup. It opens in a new tab, which is why people lose it.
Quick Find
Search box, top left of Setup
Type a few letters of what you want and it filters the whole menu. This is faster than the tree, always. Type fewer words, not more — "activity" beats "Einstein Activity Capture settings."
Object Manager
Tab at the top of Setup
Where fields, page layouts, and record types live — per object. If a kit says "on the Contact object," you are going here, not into the menu on the left.
Users
Quick Find: "users"
People, and what each can do. Almost every "it works for me but not for them" ends up here.
Lightning or Classic?
If the top of the screen is a blue bar with tabs, you are in Classic. If it is white with an app launcher of nine dots at the left, you are in Lightning. Our kits assume Lightning.
The menu moves
Salesforce ships three releases a year and things get renamed. If a kit's click path is wrong, search the label in Quick Find instead. The label survives longer than the path.
If Quick Find shows nothing
Three reasons, in the order to check them:
1. You typed too much. Try one word.
2. Your user lacks the permission that reveals it.
3. Your edition does not include it at all.
Learn Quick Find and you can stop memorizing click paths for good. Every kit, every release, same two keystrokes. Bookmark Setup itself. It opens in its own tab, and half of "I can't find it" is actually "I closed that tab."
